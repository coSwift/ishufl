Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

The short version

iShufl, provided by Jack Swift, does not collect, share or sell personal information. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only thing iShufl keeps online is the anonymous invite record described under "Inviting friends", and only if you use invite codes.

Your music and lyrics

The audio files and lyrics you import are copied into the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere. They are removed if you delete them in the app or delete the app.

Your name, skins, stickers and settings

The name you enter during setup, your custom skins, stickers, wallpapers and preferences are saved on your device only.

Photos

If you add a photo as a sticker or wallpaper, you choose it in the system photo picker, and iShufl receives only the photo you select. Cutting out a sticker's subject happens on your device.

Motion sensors

Some foil stickers shimmer as you tilt your phone. The motion data is read on your device to move the shine and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

iShufl Unlimited is a monthly subscription (with a free trial for eligible Apple Accounts) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; iShufl never sees your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Network and live radio

iShufl does not connect to any server of its own and contains no third-party code that does. Only if you use Radio, the app asks the public Radio Browser directory (radio-browser.info) for a list of stations and then plays the station's own stream. When a station announces the song that is playing, iShufl asks Apple's iTunes Search service (itunes.apple.com) for that song's album cover, sending only the artist and song title. To show what is playing on your favorite stations, iShufl briefly connects to each favorite's stream to read the song title it announces. Like any website or app that loads something from the internet, these services and the radio station can see your IP address. iShufl sends them no account, name or other personal information. Stations are provided by third parties, and some may carry mature content; you can hide a station from the player with the Report button.

Looking things up online

Some features look things up on free, public services. They run only when you choose to use them, and iShufl asks you the first time:

  • Art covers: the song's artist, album and title are sent to MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org), and the cover picture is downloaded from the Cover Art Archive (coverartarchive.org, hosted by the Internet Archive, archive.org).
  • Song details: the song's artist and title are sent to MusicBrainz.
  • Lyrics: the song's artist, title, album and length are sent to LRCLIB (lrclib.net).

Nothing else is sent, and what comes back is saved on your device only. Like any internet request, these services can see your IP address.

Inviting friends

Invite codes use iCloud. Your code is made from an anonymous ID that iCloud gives iShufl (never your name, email or Apple Account details) and is kept in iShufl's public iCloud database so a friend's code can be checked. When a friend joins with your code, a record holding your code, that friend's anonymous iCloud ID and whether they have played a song is kept there too, so your rewards can be counted and each iCloud account joins only once. These records hold no names or contact details. To have yours removed, email us your code.

Reports by email

If you choose to email a report about a radio station, the email is sent from your own mail app and contains the station's name, country and ID that you can see before sending. We use it only to look into that station.

Children

Because iShufl collects nothing, it collects no information from children either.

Changes
